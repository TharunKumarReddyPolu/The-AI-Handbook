# 📊 AI Data & Research

> Essential platforms for AI research and datasets. Whether you're fine-tuning models, exploring papers, or competing in ML challenges.

---

## 🛠️ Platforms & Resources

| Resource | Description |
|---|---|
| **[Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/)** | The GitHub of AI — models, datasets, and Spaces |
| **[Papers with Code](https://paperswithcode.com/)** | ML papers linked to code implementations and benchmarks |
| **[Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/)** | Data science competitions, datasets, and notebooks |
| **[arXiv AI](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/recent)** | Latest AI research paper preprints |
| **[Semantic Scholar](https://www.semanticscholar.org/)** | AI-powered academic paper search engine |

---

## 💡 Pro Tips

- **Hugging Face** is where the AI community shares everything — models, datasets, demos, and Spaces
- **Papers with Code** links every paper to its code implementation — no more hunting for repos
- **Kaggle** is not just competitions — their free notebooks and datasets are invaluable for learning
- **arXiv** is where breakthrough papers appear first (often weeks before formal publication)
- **Semantic Scholar** uses AI to surface relevant papers — great for literature reviews
