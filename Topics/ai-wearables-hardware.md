# ⌚ AI Wearables & Hardware

> Physical AI devices that augment your daily life — always-on AI that listens, remembers, and assists.

---

## 🔑 Key Concepts

- **Ambient AI / Second Brain Hardware** — Always-available microphones or cameras that capture conversations, transcribe them, and build a searchable personal memory
- **HUD Smart Glasses** — Lightweight eyewear with micro-LED heads-up displays for live translation, teleprompting, navigation, and visual AI assistance
- **On-Device vs. Cloud Processing** — Most wearables stream audio over Bluetooth to a phone app for transcription and LLM summarization

## 💡 Pro Tips

- **Ray-Ban Meta** dominates everyday consumer adoption because it looks like normal eyewear while adding a multimodal camera and open-ear audio
- **Plaud Note** is the easiest choice if you want physical call + meeting recording attached directly to the back of your phone
- **Omi** and **Brilliant Labs Frame** are the best choices for builders — both are open-source and let you write custom webhook integrations and apps
- **Limitless.ai** and **Plaud NotePin** excel at hands-free meeting, lecture, and brainstorming capture

---

## 🎯 When to Use What

| Need | Best Device |
|---|---|
| Hands-free camera, audio & multimodal AI | Ray-Ban Meta AI Glasses |
| Phone call + in-person meeting recording | Plaud Note |
| Discreet clip-on / pendant memory | Limitless.ai, Plaud NotePin, Bee |
| Heads-up display (teleprompter & live translation) | Even Realities G1 |
| Open-source / hackable hardware | Omi, Brilliant Labs Frame |
