# 🎨 Creative & Content Generation

> AI tools for creating images, videos, presentations, and multimedia. From photorealistic video to AI-powered slide decks.

---

## 🔑 Key Concepts

- **Text-to-Image** — Generate images from text descriptions (e.g., Midjourney, Stable Diffusion)
- **Text-to-Video** — Generate video clips from text prompts (e.g., Runway, Sora, Kling)
- **Voice Cloning** — Create synthetic voices that sound like real people
- **Character Consistency** — Maintaining the same character appearance across multiple generated scenes

## 💡 Pro Tips

- **Runway** is the industry standard for filmmakers needing precise camera and motion control
- **Kling AI** leads in photorealistic human motion — best for character-driven storytelling
- **ComfyUI** gives maximum control over Stable Diffusion pipelines via a node-based interface
- **ElevenLabs** is unmatched for voice generation and voice cloning quality
- For presentations, **Gamma** excels at modern web-style decks while **GenPPT** focuses on research-backed content
