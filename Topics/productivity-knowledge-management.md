# 📋 Productivity & Knowledge Management

> Tools for enhancing productivity, managing knowledge, and streamlining workflows. From AI meeting assistants to local-first second brains and voice typing tools.

---

## 🔑 Key Concepts

- **Second Brain / PKM (Personal Knowledge Management)** — Systems that capture, link, and surface your notes, bookmarks, and documents on demand
- **Bot-Free Meeting Capture** — Local-audio meeting recorders (like Granola and Meetily) that transcribe system audio directly without joining the call as a visible bot
- **Enterprise Knowledge Search** — Permission-aware RAG across all company apps (Slack, Drive, Jira, Notion) so employees can ask questions across organizational silos

## 💡 Pro Tips

- **Granola.ai** and **Meetily** transcribe audio directly from your laptop without putting an awkward recording bot into the Zoom/Meet participant list
- **Raycast AI** is the fastest way to use LLMs on macOS — highlight text in any app and trigger custom prompts with a single hotkey
- **Obsidian + Copilot** or **Khoj** give you a completely private, local-first "second brain" where your notes never have to leave your machine
- **GetRecall.ai** and **Readwise Reader** are unbeatable for digesting YouTube videos, long PDFs, and web articles into structured knowledge
- **Wisprflow.ai** and **Voice Ink** replace typing with 3x faster voice dictation across every app

## 🎯 When to Use What

| Need | Best Tool |
|---|---|
| Bot-free personal meeting notes | Granola.ai, Meetily (open-source) |
| Team meeting recording & CRM sync | Fathom, Fireflies.ai |
| Local-first / open-source PKM | Obsidian, Logseq, Khoj, AFFiNE, Reor |
| All-in-one team workspace AI | Notion AI, Glean (enterprise search) |
| Reading, web clipping & summarizing | Readwise Reader, GetRecall.ai |
| System-wide shortcuts & voice typing | Raycast AI, Wisprflow.ai, Voice Ink |
| Technical architecture diagrams | Eraser.io |
