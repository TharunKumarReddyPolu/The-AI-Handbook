## 💡 Pro Tips

- **GetRecall.ai** is like a "second brain" — save anything and find it instantly later
- **Logseq** is a great local-first, open-source alternative to Obsidian — outline-based note-taking with a built-in knowledge graph
- **WorldMonitor** aggregates global news with an AI dashboard — great for staying situationally aware
- **Fireflies.ai** and **Granola.ai** both transcribe meetings, but Fireflies is better for teams while Granola excels at personal note-taking
- **Wisprflow.ai** works across Mac, Windows, and iPhone — great for dictation workflows
