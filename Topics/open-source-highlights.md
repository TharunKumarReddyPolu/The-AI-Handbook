## 💡 Pro Tips

- **Ollama + llama.cpp** are the backbone of the local AI movement — Ollama wraps llama.cpp with a friendly CLI
- **Dify** is the most complete open-source platform for building LLM apps with RAG, agents, and model management
- **n8n** has evolved from a workflow tool to a powerful AI agent builder
- Fork and contribute to these repos — the AI open-source community is one of the most active on GitHub
