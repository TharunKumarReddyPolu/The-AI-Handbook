# 💼 AI for Business & Commerce

> AI tools for GTM, sales, legal, finance, customer support, and e-commerce. Automate your business operations with AI.

---

## 🔑 Key Concepts

- **AI GTM & Enrichment** — Chaining multiple data providers with LLMs to research accounts, score leads, and draft hyper-personalized outreach
- **Autonomous Support Agents** — Action-taking customer service agents that verify identity, issue refunds, and update orders under strict business policies
- **Vertical Enterprise AI** — Domain-specialized platforms (legal, finance, revenue intelligence) grounded in firm-specific documents and compliance controls

## 💡 Pro Tips

- **Clay** is the gold standard for modern GTM engineering — use its AI research agent ("Claygent") to scrape and qualify leads before sending a single email
- **Fin by Intercom** and **Sierra** lead in customer support because they connect directly to backend systems to resolve issues, not just deflect them
- **Crystal Knows** and **Gong** pair well for sales teams — understand your prospect's communication style before the call and automatically analyze deal risk after
- **Madgicx** automates ad optimization across platforms — set it and let AI handle the creative and bidding
- Building an agent that charges users or manages subscriptions? Use the official **Stripe Agent Toolkit** (`stripe/ai`)
