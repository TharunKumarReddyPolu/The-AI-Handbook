## 💡 Pro Tips

- **Start with Latent Space** if you're an engineer — it's the highest-signal show for builders
- **Lex Fridman + Dwarkesh** together cover both breadth (Lex) and depth (Dwarkesh) of frontier AI
- **Hard Fork** is the best show to share with non-technical friends and colleagues
- **The AI Daily Brief** is your morning coffee companion — 10 minutes to stay fully informed
- **No Priors** is essential if you're building a startup or evaluating AI investments
- Subscribe to 2-3 max and rotate — the AI podcast space moves fast
